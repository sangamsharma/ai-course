# Product

## Register

hybrid

## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary audience (equal split):** Elderly learners (60+, limited tech experience) and non-technical adults (30–60, used AI once or twice for emails). Both groups share the same need: practical AI skills without jargon, overwhelm, or condescension.

**Context:** At home, on phone or computer, during spare moments. Many are learning alone. They arrive curious but skeptical — they've heard AI is "for tech people" and aren't sure they belong.

**Job to be done:** Learn to use ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini for real everyday tasks — writing, planning, learning, organizing — without feeling lost or stupid.

## Product Purpose

Teach AI tools in a way that's relevant to daily life. Not theory, not hype, not "10x productivity." Practical skills you can use tomorrow. The platform exists to remove the intimidation barrier between normal people and AI.

**Success:** A user finishes a module, uses AI confidently for something real that same day, and comes back for more because it felt easy and useful.

## Positioning

AI skills taught like a patient friend, not a lecture hall. Every screen says: "You belong here. This is for you. Let's do something useful together."

## Conversion & proof

- **Primary CTA:** Take the onboarding diagnostic quiz — answer a few friendly questions, get a personalized learning path ("Find your starting point").
- **Secondary CTA:** Browse modules directly — for visitors who prefer self-directed discovery.
- **The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds:** "AI isn't just for tech people. Let's learn something useful, together."
- **Belief ladder:**
  1. It's worth my time — I'll learn things I can actually use tomorrow (not abstract theory).
  2. AI is for me — this isn't just for tech people. I belong here and can learn this.
  3. This won't be overwhelming — bite-sized, friendly, safe to try. No judgment.
- **Proof on hand:** Course content itself serves as proof — sample lessons visible before commitment, real-world scenario examples, clear "what you'll be able to do" statements per module.

## Brand Personality

Clear, practical, trustworthy. The voice of a knowledgeable friend who respects your time and never talks down to you. No hype. No jargon. No "revolutionize your workflow."

**Reference:** Headspace — calm, warm, inviting. Simple navigation, gentle colors, feels safe and supportive. Bite-sized sessions. Progress that feels encouraging, not gamified-pressure.

**Emotional goal:** The user feels capable, not marketed-to. Respected, not upsold. "I can do this" — not "this app is impressive."

## Anti-references

- **AI hype landing pages** — no gradient text, no purple-to-blue defaults, no overpromising "transform your life" language, no dark-mode-default "AI tool" aesthetic
- **Corporate SaaS** — no cold enterprise energy, no "platform" speak, no dashboard-as-personality
- **Academic textbook** — no dense walls of text, no formal lecture tone, nothing that feels like homework

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell.** Every concept demonstrated with real examples the user can try immediately. No abstract theory without practice.
2. **Respect the learner.** No jargon, no condescension, no overwhelm. The user is smart — the interface should be simple.
3. **Practical first.** Every lesson answers "what can I do with this today?" If it's not useful tomorrow, it doesn't belong in the course.
4. **Calm clarity.** Headspace-like warmth and simplicity. Breathing room. One thing at a time. The interface never shouts.
5. **Safe to explore.** Privacy-first by design. No real data collected. Clear boundaries around what AI can and can't do. The user always knows where they stand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AAA targeted (current baseline is AA with 18px body, reduced motion, skip link, focus rings)
- Large touch targets for elderly learners on mobile
- Plain language as default — reading level appropriate for broad audience
- No time limits on exercises
- High contrast beyond AA minimums
- Color not the sole indicator of any state
- Reduced motion respected globally
